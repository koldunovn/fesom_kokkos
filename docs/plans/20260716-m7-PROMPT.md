**0.2 — `measured_total − modelled_part` IS NOT A MEASUREMENT (L88).** Its error bar is the model's, and
you do not know that. **If you want to know what is left, measure what is left.** The honest form of a
decomposition is a **controlled differential**: change ONE thing, re-measure, and let the *difference of
two measurements* name the part. (That is how both the eta_n bug and the mEVP bug were proven.)

**0.3 — NO SERIAL GATE CAN VALIDATE A COHERENCE INVARIANT (L86).** On Serial `.d()` **is** `.h()`, so
every rail is a no-op and every clobber is a self-assignment. **The standalone CUDA fidelity gate costs
42 seconds. It is the ONLY gate for any `sync_*`/`modify_*`/rail change.** Corollary (rule 0.3-bis):
before deleting a rail because "both sides are device kernels", **ask who put the initial value there.**
