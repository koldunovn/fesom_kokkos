## 3. 🔴 THE FOREGROUND LEVER — **H.8 "LAZYSNAP"**: syncs at step cadence serving a reader at snapshot cadence

**Pre-seeded with verified facts (I read the code; verify, then build):**

1. **The 9-copy ice OUT rail (`fesom_ice.cpp:934-941`) fires EVERY STEP** — it is in every trace,
   ~4.9 ms PCIe + ~2.3 ms gap. ICERAILS added it deliberately as "the ONE OUT rail that replaces all 67";
   its comment names the consumers: `srfoce_u/v` → the HOST `oce_fluxes_mom` (**dead**, ICEFLUXDEV is on);
   `h_ice/h_snow` → `FESOM_DIAG_MICE` only (**dead in production** — the comment admits "no production
   I/O reader"); **`a/m/ms` + `uice/vice` → the I/O SNAPSHOT GATHER** (`fesom_io.cpp:374-381`,
   `.h_checked()`).
2. 🔴 **THE SNAPSHOT GATHER NEVER RUNS IN THE BENCHMARKS.** `fesom_main.cpp:962-967`: the A/B jobs pass
   `snap_every_cli = -1` → `snap_every = 0` → `fesom_io_write_snapshot` is never called. **The rail
   serves a reader that runs at snapshot cadence (or never), at step cadence.** In production (monthly
   output) it is ~10⁴× too often.
3. **The writer already guards itself**: every gather source goes through `Field::h_checked()`, which
   **ABORTS under `-DFESOM_KK_SYNCCHECK` if the field is device-authoritative** at the read. So moving
   the syncs INTO `fesom_io_write_snapshot` (sync just before gather) is **safe by construction** — a
   missed one is a loud abort in the SYNCCHECK build, not silent garbage.
4. **The same question applies to the OCEAN fields in the gather list** (`T, S, eta_n, w, uv,
   density_m_rho0, bvfreq, pgf_x/y, Kv, Av` — `fesom_io.cpp:369-381`): find each one's per-step
   `sync_host()` in the step driver and ask *"does any consumer other than the snapshot need this at
   step cadence?"* The census pair-table rows `hbar→timestep` (3.9 PCIe) and `ssh_solve→update_vel`
   (1.6 PCIe) look like exactly this class. **This is why the lever might be ~2×** the ice's 7 ms.

**Shape of the change** (BULKTAIL is the template, `93d434d`): knob `FESOM_SPEED_LAZYSNAP`; under it,
delete the per-step syncs and put explicit `sync_host()` for every gathered field at the top of
`fesom_io_write_snapshot` (and any other genuine host reader you find — audit `FESOM_DIAG_*` blocks);
**abort on incompatible toggles rather than silently degrading (L80)**; mind the mEVP/options paths
(L91) and who-wrote-the-IC (rule 0.3). **Cross-step trap to re-derive:** the `:931-933` comment says the
rail leaves a/m/ms+uice/vice `Synced`, "exactly what the NEXT step's EVP kernel needs … Do not remove
without re-deriving." Deleting the sync leaves them **Device-authoritative** — device stays current, so
kernels are fine — but any *non-blessed* `modify_host()+sync_device()` downstream becomes a clobber ⇒
the knob must **require ICERAILS+ICEFLUXDEV+FLUXDEV** like BULKTAIL does.
**Size it from the h9 census (26258712) FIRST** — gap ms, not bytes (L89) — and pre-register **a floor**
