# M11 under the deterministic initial condition — what has to be re-run, and why

**Context.** M13 found that FESOM's climatology hole-filler (`extrap_nod3D`) produces a
*different initial condition for every decomposition*, and that this — not any solver — is what
made partitions blow up. The fix is `FESOM_IC_EXTRAP=det`
(`docs/CG_BLOWUPS_M13.md` in the main checkout); it is on this branch as 4685cce + 7a084e9.
The user's decision is that every partition-exploration run now carries it.

This page is the M11-side evaluation: which of the campaign's results the change invalidates,
which it leaves alone, and in what order the affected ones are being re-measured.

## 1. What the change can and cannot touch

The knob changes exactly one thing: the state the model starts from. So:

**Affected — anything downstream of a cold start.**

1. *Stability verdicts.* Every partition this campaign declared unusable was declared so on the
   strength of a run whose initial condition was an artefact of that same partition. Those
   verdicts do not measure the partition.
2. *Speed numbers.* Two partitions of one mesh were integrating **different problems**, so they
   ran different transients, took different numbers of CG iterations, and therefore took
   different amounts of time. How much of a measured gain is that, and how much is the
   decomposition? Unknown until re-measured — this is the part of the campaign nobody suspected.
3. *Accuracy / disturbance tiers.* The yardstick was the spread across seed-control
   re-rolls of the stock recipe. Under the legacy fill those controls differed in their initial
   conditions as well as their decomposition, so the yardstick was partly measuring the artefact.
   Under det all legs start from bitwise-identical state and the spread becomes pure model
   response — it should shrink, sharpening the comparison in *both* directions.

**Not affected — anything measured offline or without a cold start.**

* The whole partition-quality analysis: edge cut, communication volume, `nbr_max`, halo size,
  element replication, placement, the engine comparison, the ordering survey, the Pareto work.
  No model is run to produce any of it.
* The `check_partitioning` isolated-node defect (Finding 9) and its fix, and both upstream PRs.
* The four promoted meshes **as artefacts** — a `dist_N` file is unchanged by this. Their
  *evidence* was recorded under the legacy fill, so it needs re-stating, not the partitions.
* The partitioner itself, its knob gate, and the null tests.

## 2. Certification of the re-pinned binary (done)

Every re-run needs a binary that has the knob; the frozen `h17` pins do not. Built from this
branch tip and installed at `/work/ab0995/a270088/port2/m11/bin/det1/`:

| binary | md5 |
|---|---|
| `fesom_port_serial` | `07d982e0757e8e258a479485840d897c` |
| `fesom_port_cuda`   | `1f16832befb6039b4f71f6b1b1785836` |

`jobs/m11_det_bin_gate.sh` (job **26961161**) certifies it with two legs, both required:

* **knob off → byte-identical** to the certified M6/M7 Serial baseline. This is what licenses the
  claim that the only thing separating a det re-run from the original is the initial condition.
* **knob on → different**, with the announce line present. Without this leg a binary that ignored
  the environment variable would pass the first leg and every "det" re-run would silently be a
  legacy re-run (the dead-knob trap, L80).

Both passed. `jobs/m11_race_part.sh`, `m11_race_part_gpu.sh`, `m11_gate_part.sh` and
`m11_gate_part_gpu.sh` now default to these binaries and to `FESOM_IC_EXTRAP=det`, print the
setting in their header, and flag any leg whose log lacks the announce line.

## 3. The census that sizes the problem per mesh (`jobs/m11_ic_census.sh`)

Before re-running a five-mesh campaign it is worth knowing which meshes were contaminated at
all. The step-0 snapshot *is* the initial condition after the fill, so writing it for several
partitions of one mesh and differencing them measures the artefact directly, with no integration:

| job | mesh | ranks | legs |
|---|---|--:|---|
| 26961259 | CORE2 | 512 | base · `a5_u30` · seed control |
| 26961260 | fArc | 2048 | base · Mt-KaHyPar · seed control |
| 26961261 | dars | 2048 | base · `a4m` · **`dars_seed660013`** (the F45 killer) |
| 26961262 | NG5 | 2048 | base · `a4m` · `a4m_seedb` |

Each runs every leg twice, once per fill, and reports the difference at step 0 (the initial
condition itself) and at step 1 (has one step already amplified it). Reading:

* legacy legs identical → that mesh's existing results are uncontaminated and stand;
* legacy legs differ → everything cold-started on that mesh compared different problems;
* det legs identical → the fix holds at this mesh and rank count.

### Result: every mesh measured so far is contaminated

Largest difference between two partitions of the same mesh, at step 0:

| mesh / ranks | fill | salinity | temperature | ice concentration | after one step (salt) |
|---|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| CORE2 512 | legacy | **27.4 PSU** | **7.0 °C** | 0.9 | 27.4 PSU |
| CORE2 512 | det | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1.7e-13 PSU |
| fArc 2048 | legacy | **26.0 PSU** | **9.0 °C** | 0.9 | 26.0 PSU |
| fArc 2048 | det | 0 | 0 | 0 | 6.5e-13 PSU |
| dars 2048 | legacy | **27.0 PSU** | **7.6 °C** | 0.9 | (det legs re-running) |
| dars 2048, F45 killer partition | legacy | **27.0 PSU** | 6.4 °C | 0.9 | — |

The dars row includes `dars_seed660013`, the stock-recipe seed re-roll whose blow-up at step 14
became Finding 45 and made the adoption screen universal. It starts 27 PSU away from the shipped
partition's initial state, which closes the M11 side of that finding directly.

CORE2 was the mesh assumed to have almost no dummy coverage, so this settles the scope: the
re-run is the whole board, both backends, not a repair of NG5 and dars.

One artefact of the instrument, worth writing down so nobody chases it: `pgf_x`/`pgf_y` differ
between partitions by the *same* amount under both fills, so the pressure-gradient field in a
step-0 snapshot is written before it is computed and carries no information.

behind them.

### Conditional on the census

CORE2 and fArc points (races and gates) are re-run if their census shows the initial condition
moved between partitions; if it did not, their numbers stand as measured and only their
provenance needs a note.

### Accuracy, everywhere

The 20-step gates are cheap (about ten minutes at 2048 ranks) and the envelope re-derivation is
the part most likely to change conclusions, so every gated point gets one under det with at
least three demonstrated-distinct seed controls: dars 2048, NG5 2048 (never gated — it was a
null), fArc 2048, CORE2 864, CORE2 512, then the four GPU points.

## 5. Standing rules that still apply

Ladder dt in every job (CORE2 1800 · fArc 900 · dars 120 · NG5 180). `BIN=` pinned and md5-checked.
`ARMS` passed in the environment with a bare `--export=ALL`. At least three controls, demonstrated
distinct. Report the median as well as the min whenever the spreads differ. Sixteen GPU nodes is
the cap. Nothing is a recommendation until it has been screened at 3,000 steps at its rank count.
